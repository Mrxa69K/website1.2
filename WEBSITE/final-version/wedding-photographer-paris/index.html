Questions people ask

Can you photograph just the town hall ceremony?

Yes. The town hall package covers 3 to 4 hours: the ceremony, family photos and a short couple session afterwards.

How far ahead should we book?

For a Saturday between May and September, as early as possible. For a town hall ceremony on a weekday, a few weeks is often enough.

Do you travel outside Paris?

Yes, around Paris and Île-de-France, châteaux included. Tell me your venue in the order envelope and I will confirm.

We are a bilingual or multicultural couple. Is that a problem?

Quite the opposite. I speak French, English and Arabic, so both families can talk to me directly on the day.

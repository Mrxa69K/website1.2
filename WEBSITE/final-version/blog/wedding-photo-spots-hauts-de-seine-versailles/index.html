Wedding photos in the Hauts-de-Seine: 6 spots, Boulogne to Versailles

Getting married in Boulogne, Issy, Meudon or Versailles? The most beautiful backdrops right next to the town hall for your couple photos.

By Melissa, photographer in Paris

In this article

  1. Île Saint-Germain park, Issy-les-Moulineaux
  2. The Observatory terrace, Meudon
  3. Domaine national de Saint-Cloud
  4. Parc de Sceaux
  5. Boulogne-Billancourt: the town hall and Île Seguin
  6. Versailles, beyond the château

Getting married at the town hall of Boulogne-Billancourt, Issy-les-Moulineaux, Meudon or Versailles, and wondering where to take your couple photos right after? Good news: the west of Paris has beautiful backdrops, often calmer than the ones in Paris.

Here are six spots I recommend, with the best time to go. Some estates require permission for a professional photo session: we always check together before the day.

1. Île Saint-Germain park, Issy-les-Moulineaux

Practical Perfect right after the Issy town hall

Parc de l'île Saint-Germain, 92130 Issy-les-MoulineauxOpen in Maps

An island in the Seine, wide lawns, tree-lined paths and Jean Dubuffet's Tour aux figures, a monumental black and white sculpture that makes a very graphic background.

It is a local park, so no tourist crowds: we can take our time, pose without pressure and take lovely photos by the water.

2. The Observatory terrace, Meudon

Practical The best view of Paris, especially at sunset

Terrasse de l'Observatoire, 92190 MeudonOpen in Maps

A long, tree-lined terrace up on the hill, with all of Paris at your feet and the Eiffel Tower in the distance. It is the perfect backdrop for a couple photo with the city behind you, without crossing the ring road.

Go at the end of the day: the golden light comes from the front and the sky turns pink above Paris.

3. Domaine national de Saint-Cloud

Practical For wide open spaces and French formal gardens

Domaine national de Saint-Cloud, 92210 Saint-CloudOpen in Maps

A huge park with the Grande Cascade, endless avenues and viewpoints over Paris. It feels like a château, without going far.

It is a national estate: permission may be required for a professional session. I will help you check before you book.

4. Parc de Sceaux

Practical In spring, the cherry blossoms

Domaine départemental de Sceaux, 92330 SceauxOpen in Maps

The château, the Grand Canal and gardens designed by Le Nôtre: it is one of the most beautiful parks in the Île-de-France. In April, the park's Japanese cherry trees draw big crowds, so we go early in the morning.

The rest of the year, the avenues and pools make for very elegant photos, perfect for a wedding.

5. Boulogne-Billancourt: the town hall and Île Seguin

Practical Art deco and modern architecture

La Seine Musicale, île Seguin, 92100 Boulogne-BillancourtOpen in Maps

Boulogne-Billancourt's town hall, designed by Tony Garnier in the 1930s, has an art deco façade that instantly gives character to your photos as you walk out.

For a more modern look, Île Seguin and La Seine Musicale offer contemporary lines with the Seine all around, just a few minutes away.

6. Versailles, beyond the château

Practical Plan the permission for the estate

Quartier Notre-Dame, 78000 VersaillesOpen in Maps

Getting married in Versailles is a dream, and the château park is a unique backdrop. A professional session inside the estate usually requires permission: plan it well before the wedding.

If you want to keep it simple, the streets of the Notre-Dame quarter, with their old façades and squares, already have a very royal feel, close to the town hall.

Good to know

  • Plan 30 to 45 minutes of couple photos right after the town hall: it is often the only calm moment of the day.
  • Choose a spot less than 15 minutes from the town hall or the venue, so you don't lose time on the road.
  • Check the rules of the place (permission, opening hours) a few weeks before. I will help you.
  • Have a plan B for rain: a porch, an arcade or the town hall itself.

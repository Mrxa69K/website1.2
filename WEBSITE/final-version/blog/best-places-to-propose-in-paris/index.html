The 6 most beautiful places to propose in Paris (from a proposal photographer)

Trocadéro, Bir-Hakeim, the Louvre, Montmartre: where to kneel, when to go and how to avoid the crowds.

By Melissa

I photograph proposals in Paris all year round, hidden in the crowd with my camera. The question I get most often comes before the ring: where should I do it? Here are the six spots I recommend, what each one looks like on camera, and the time of day that makes it work.

1. Trocadéro: the classic Eiffel Tower view

The esplanade between the two wings of the Palais de Chaillot gives you the whole Eiffel Tower behind you. It is the most famous view in Paris, which is also its weak point: it is busy from late morning to midnight.

Go at sunrise if you want it almost to yourselves, or after dark for the lights. After dusk, the tower sparkles for five minutes at the start of every hour: a proposal timed on the hour is pure magic in photos.

2. Bir-Hakeim bridge: iron, arches and the tower

The upper deck of the Bir-Hakeim bridge has a central walkway framed by iron columns, with the Eiffel Tower just next to it. It feels more intimate than Trocadéro and the lines of the bridge make beautiful frames.

Early morning is the calmest time. During the day, other couples and photographers come here too, so we pick a spot between two columns where nobody will walk through the moment.

3. The Louvre arcades: elegant, and perfect when it rains

The arcades around the Louvre courtyards give long stone perspectives and soft, even light. It is my favourite plan B: if it rains, you stay dry and the photos stay beautiful.

Morning is best for quiet arcades. The glass pyramid is close by if you want a second backdrop for the couple photos afterwards.

4. Montmartre and the Wall of Love

In Square Jehan-Rictus, near Abbesses metro, the Wall of Love (le mur des je t'aime) shows "I love you" written 311 times in 250 languages. Entry is free; the square's opening hours depend on the season, so check before you plan an early or late moment.

Then walk up to the Sacré-Cœur for the view over Paris, or through the small streets around Place du Tertre. Montmartre suits couples who want something more bohemian than the Eiffel Tower.

5. Pont Alexandre III: golden statues and the Seine

The most ornate bridge in Paris, with its gilded statues and lamps, the Seine below and a view towards the Invalides and the Eiffel Tower. It is wide, so even with people around there is room to find a clean background.

Sunset gives it a warm glow, and the lamps look lovely once they light up.

6. The banks of the Seine at sunset

For a simpler, softer proposal, a walk along the quays at golden hour works beautifully: the river, the bridges and the light do the work. Île Saint-Louis and the quays near Notre-Dame are lovely for this.

It's also the easiest plan for a surprise: a walk by the river doesn't raise any suspicion.

Good to know

  • Timing: sunrise for empty spots, golden hour for warm light, and after dark for the Eiffel Tower lights (sparkle: five minutes at the start of every hour).
  • Agree on a signal with your photographer, and tell me exactly where you will stop. I arrive early and blend in.
  • Keep the ring in a front pocket, not in a bag, so you are not searching for it at the big moment.
  • Have a rain plan: the Louvre arcades are covered and just as beautiful.
  • Want lights, candles, a carpet or the "MARRY ME" letters? I can set up the decoration for you.

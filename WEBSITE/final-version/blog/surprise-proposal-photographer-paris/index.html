Surprise proposal photographer Paris

Capture your surprise marriage proposal in Paris with natural and candid photography.

By Melissa, photographer in Paris

In this article

  1. Choosing the right location for your surprise
  2. Keeping the secret until the big day
  3. Coordinating with your photographer
  4. Packages and fast delivery

Planning to pop the big question in the most romantic city in the world? It is a wonderful adventure that requires a little preparation, especially if you want to keep the element of surprise until the very last moment.

As a photographer in Paris and the western suburbs, I am here to capture every emotion of this unique moment authentically and at gentle prices.

1. Choosing the right location for your surprise

The setting is essential for a successful proposal. Paris is full of stunning backdrops, but some locations lend themselves better to discretion and privacy.

For a great view without the crowds, I recommend spots like Bir-Hakeim bridge, Allée des Cygnes, Avenue de Camoëns, Square du Vert-Galant, or Palais-Royal. I generally avoid Trocadéro for proposals due to the heavy crowds.

  • Choose quiet times, like early in the morning, for more privacy.
  • Check if a permit is needed depending on the chosen public location.
  • Have a backup plan in case of bad weather.

2. Keeping the secret until the big day

The biggest challenge of a surprise proposal is making sure your partner suspects nothing. Good preparation is your best ally.

You can, for example, present the photo session as a simple romantic gift or a classic couple shoot. This way, your significant other will be relaxed and ready without guessing that you are about to pull out a ring.

3. Coordinating with your photographer

On the big day, timing and discretion are key. We will define a precise meeting point and a visual signal together so I know exactly when you drop to one knee.

I blend into the background to capture the full surprise, tears of joy, smiles, and hugs, and then we take a few minutes for lovely, relaxed couple portraits.

4. Packages and fast delivery

Since you will want to share the great news with your loved ones as soon as possible, I offer tailored packages with very fast delivery.

I offer an express 30-minute proposal for 150 € with 10 photos or 200 € for 20+ photos, delivered in 24 hours. For a 1-hour session, prices start at 290 € with 20+ photos delivered in 24 hours. If you wish for a complete setup, the proposal with decoration starts at 690 €.

Good to know

  • Discuss the scenario with your photographer beforehand.
  • Choose a location that has meaning for your relationship.
  • Prepare a few words to say when you present the ring.
  • Enjoy the moment without stressing about the photos.

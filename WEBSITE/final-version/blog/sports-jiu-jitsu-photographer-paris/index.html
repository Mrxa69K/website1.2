Sports and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Photographer in Paris

Dynamic and sharp photos for your sports competitions, training sessions, and portraits in Paris.

By Melissa, photographer in Paris

In this article

  1. Why hire a sports photographer in Paris?
  2. Photographing Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: capturing the gentle art
  3. Packages and rates for sports photography
  4. How a sports photo session works

Sports represent passion, dedication, and pushing limits. Whether it is a demanding martial art like Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu or another athletic discipline in Paris and Western Paris, capturing the moment requires precision, responsiveness, and a deep understanding of movement.

As a photographer passionate about movement and athletic action, I put my lens at the service of athletes, clubs, and sports enthusiasts. My goal is to capture the intensity of a fight, the technical precision of a move, and the raw emotion of a tournament.

Whether you want to update your club website, feed your social channels, or keep memorable milestones of your athletic journey, I offer tailored packages with a professional and natural finish.

1. Why hire a sports photographer in Paris?

Sports photography requires technical skill. Between fast movements, complex indoor lighting, and the necessity not to disturb athletes during performance, every detail matters.

Hiring a professional photographer ensures sharp images captured at the exact right instant. I capture the determination in your eyes, muscle tension, and collective energy.

These pictures allow you to showcase your daily effort, promote your events, or simply document your personal victories and progress.

2. Photographing Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu: capturing the gentle art

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a fascinating discipline to photograph. From guard passes and submissions to takedowns and ground control, every second holds technical detail and intense emotion.

BJJ photography requires understanding the rhythm of fights to anticipate transitions and position oneself around the mat without interfering with spars. I focus on tight angles and dynamic compositions to highlight the strength and flow of this martial art.

Whether during an open tournament, a seminar, or a regular club training session, I capture the authentic vibe of the Jiu-Jitsu community.

3. Packages and rates for sports photography

I offer clear and affordable pricing for individual athletes as well as sports organizations:

All selected photos are carefully edited individually and delivered in a private online gallery, ready for high-definition download within a few days.

  • Individual sports portrait and action session: starting at 100 €.
  • Sports event or tournament coverage: starting at 300 € for 2 hours (60+ delivered photos).
  • Half-day package (500 €) or full-day package (750 €) for clubs and major events.

4. How a sports photo session works

Before the shoot, we discuss your goals: key moments to cover, desired photo style (action, portrait, behind-the-scenes), and location in Paris or Hauts-de-Seine.

During the event or session, I work discreetly. I use ambient light without intrusive flashes to maintain the athletes' concentration.

After the event, you will receive access to your private online gallery to easily download all your high-resolution images.

Good to know

  • Prepare clean gear and uniforms ahead of the shoot.
  • Inform me in advance about specific techniques or key moments you want captured.
  • Check photography permissions with your gym or tournament organizers.
  • Embrace natural emotions, from intense focus to post-training smiles.

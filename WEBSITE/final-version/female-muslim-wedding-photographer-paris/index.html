In many families, some moments of the wedding happen among women: the bride getting ready, the henna night, the women's hall. Being a woman means I can be there, discreetly, without anyone feeling uncomfortable.

I speak Arabic, French and English. I understand both families, I know how to wait for the right moment during the couple's entrance or the outfit changes, and I organise group photos quickly without rushing anyone.

Your photos stay yours: a private, password-protected gallery, and nothing is posted on my social media without your written consent. If some guests prefer not to be photographed, we plan for it together before the day.

  • A female photographer for the women-only moments (getting ready, henna, women's hall)
  • The henna night, town hall and celebration, depending on the package
  • Respect for modesty: we agree together on what can be photographed
  • A private, password-protected gallery, nothing published without your consent

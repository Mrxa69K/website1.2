Questions people ask

What time of day is best?

Early morning for empty streets and soft light, or the hour before sunset for golden light. Both are lovely; mornings are quieter.

What should we wear?

Something you feel good in. Plain colours and soft tones work best; avoid big logos. A second outfit is fine if you want a change of look.

We're shy in front of a camera. Is that OK?

Totally. Most couples are. I talk you through every step and keep things light, so it feels like a walk, not a shoot.

Can I book a solo portrait?

Yes. Solo portrait sessions start at €150: portraits for yourself, your social media or your work.

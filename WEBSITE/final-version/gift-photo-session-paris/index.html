Questions people ask

How does it work?

Choose the session and fill in the order envelope, ticking “It’s a gift”. I reply within 24 hours with the PayPal payment link, and I send you the voucher as a PDF as soon as the payment arrives.

How long is the voucher valid?

12 months from the date of purchase. They choose their date freely during that time, depending on my availability.

How do I pay?

By PayPal: I send you a secure payment link after your request.

Can I gift another session?

Yes, every session can be a gift: couple, portrait, proposal, wedding, concert or sport. Choose it in the order envelope. If you prefer to let them choose, write the amount you want to give in the message.

Is it too late for Christmas?

I reply within 24 hours and send the voucher as soon as the payment arrives, so you can order even a few days before Christmas and print it yourself.

Where can the session take place?

Anywhere in Paris and the west of Paris, as far as Versailles: Bir-Hakeim, the Louvre, the banks of the Seine, a park. We choose together depending on the season and what they love.

Many couples get married in the 92: at the town hall of Boulogne-Billancourt, Issy-les-Moulineaux, Meudon or Versailles. I come to you, from the town hall steps to the reception, to photograph the ceremony, the families and a quiet moment for the two of you right after.

The west of Paris has beautiful backdrops for couple photos: the Île Saint-Germain in Issy, the terrace of the Meudon Observatory with its view of the Eiffel Tower, the Domaine national de Saint-Cloud, the Parc de Sceaux or the banks of the Seine in Boulogne. We pick the one that suits you, and the time of day with the softest light.

Some places, like the Versailles estate or certain parks, require permission for a professional photo session. We check everything together before you book, so the day itself runs without surprises.

Yes, across the whole Hauts-de-Seine (92): Boulogne-Billancourt, Issy-les-Moulineaux, Meudon, Sèvres, Saint-Cloud, Sceaux, Rueil-Malmaison, Neuilly-sur-Seine and the towns around. Just mention the place in the order envelope.

And Versailles?

Yes, I go as far as Versailles and the towns around it, for a town hall wedding, a couple session or a proposal. For the château estate, permission may be needed: we look into it together.

How much is a photographer for a town hall wedding in the 92?

My town hall package starts at €250 for 2 hours and goes up to €400 depending on length. A half day is €500 and a full day €750. Photography only, no video.

Where can we take couple photos near Boulogne and Issy?

The Île Saint-Germain in Issy, the Meudon Observatory terrace for the view over Paris, the Domaine national de Saint-Cloud or the banks of the Seine. I will advise you depending on the season and the time.

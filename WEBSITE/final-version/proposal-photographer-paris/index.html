Questions people ask

Where are the best places to propose in Paris?

My favourite is the Bir-Hakeim bridge, with the Allée des Cygnes just below it. Avenue de Camoëns, Square du Vert-Galant and the Palais-Royal are lovely too. I avoid Trocadéro: it is far too crowded for such a personal moment. Read my full guide on the blog.

How will my partner not notice you?

We agree on the exact spot and a signal beforehand. I arrive early, blend in with the other visitors and start shooting from a distance, then come closer once the yes is said.

What if it rains?

We watch the forecast together. We can move the time, or pick a covered spot such as the Louvre arcades.

When do we get the photos?

Within 24 hours, in a private online gallery you can download and share with family and friends.

Do you also do engagement photos?

Yes. If you are already engaged, the couple session (1h30, 25+ photos, from €250) is perfect for engagement photos in Paris, by the Eiffel Tower or in a quieter spot.
